HOW IT WORKS

Our application leverages a highly optimized AI architecture to deliver fast, robust, and privacy-preserving gesture recognition entirely on your device.

📸

1. Edge Computing

We access your webcam securely via the browser's MediaDevices API. All processing happens locally on your machine—no video data is ever sent to the cloud, ensuring total privacy.

🖐️

2. MediaPipe Tracking

Google's MediaPipe framework runs a lightning-fast neural network to detect hands and extract 21 3D landmarks in real-time.

📐

3. Heuristic Analysis

Instead of relying on basic image classification, we use spatial mathematics to analyze the exact joint positions and angles of your fingers, identifying gestures with incredible precision.
